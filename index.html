Omukazi W'omutima Organization

Coffee services · Kanungu, Uganda

Omukazi W'omutima Organization (OWO) works with coffee farmers in Kanungu District. We hull and sun-dry coffee, buy FAQ coffee from farmers, and sell roasted coffee and coffee husks.

OWO Coffee app

OWO Coffee is our internal Android app. Our staff and field agents use it to record transactions and print receipts, sales acknowledgements and payment acknowledgements. Only people the company has registered can use it. Staff sign in with their Google account.

Every OWO document has a QR code. Scanning it opens a page that shows whether the document is genuine.
