Privacy Policy

OWO Coffee app · Omukazi W'Omutima Limited

Effective date: 15 October 2026

This policy explains how Omukazi W'Omutima Limited ("OWO", "we") handles information in the OWO Coffee Android app and its online document checks. The app is an internal tool for our staff and field agents.

1. Information from your Google account

When you sign in with Google, the app receives only your e-mail address and a sign-in token that confirms it. We use the e-mail address for one purpose: to check that you are on OWO's list of authorised users and to find your role. We do not ask Google for your contacts, files, calendar or any other data. We do not keep your Google password and never see it.

2. Business records you enter

Staff use the app to record coffee transactions. These records can include farmers' and customers' names, phone numbers, national ID numbers, locations, quantities and amounts paid. The records are stored in OWO's own Google Sheets spreadsheet, which only authorised OWO staff can open. We keep each record with the name of the staff member who entered it and the date and time. This audit trail lets us check our records.

3. Information stored on the phone

Transactions entered without internet are kept on the phone until they can be sent. They are removed from the queue once the server confirms it has received them. The app also remembers the server address, your sign-in and your printer choice. Signing out removes your sign-in from the phone.

4. Bluetooth and printing

The app uses Bluetooth only to connect to receipt printers you choose. It does not use Bluetooth to find or track your location.

5. Document verification (QR codes)

Each printed document carries a QR code with a random check code and no personal information. Anyone who scans it sees whether the document is genuine, its number, date, type and status, and the name of the person on it partly hidden.

6. Sharing

We do not sell personal information or use it for advertising. We share it only when the law requires us to, or with our professional advisers under a duty of confidence. Records are hosted on Google's services, under Google's own terms.

7. How long we keep records

We keep business records for as long as we need them for our operations, accounting and legal obligations. When a staff member leaves, their access is removed from the list of authorised users.

8. Your choices and rights

You can ask to see or correct the information we hold about you, or ask us questions about this policy, using the contact details below.

9. Changes

If we change this policy, we will post the new version on this page with a new effective date.
